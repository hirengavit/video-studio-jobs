# Editorial review: Payment of postage, stamps and postal stationery

Reviewed all ten English source sections against the draft, the Hindi translation for wording, the 26-point channel checklist, and the three earlier lessons for continuity. The English owner-supplied material controls factual differences. No outside rule text was used.

## Problems

### Opening: obligation, modes and the two kinds of recovery
> Postage आम तौर पर पहले देना होता है।

“Usually” weakens a mandatory prepayment rule. The opening also gave no clear base for the doubling and could sound like a rule for international mail.

**Fix:** Postage पहले देना ज़रूरी है, जब तक तय छूट न हो।

### Source coverage: statutory stamp forms
> Stamp चिपकाया हुआ हो सकता है, छपा हुआ, या उभरा हुआ।
> या item पर किसी और तरह दिखाया गया।

The explanation skips two included forms (embedded and impressed); the source specifically lists both.

**Fix:** Stamp चिपका हो सकता है, छपा हो सकता है या उभरा हुआ। वो item में बना हो सकता है, उस पर छाप हो सकती है, या किसी और तरह दिख सकता है।

### Repeated traps and fact density
> पर ये नियम हर sender को बिना पैसे post करने की छूट नहीं देते।
> Exam में trap यहीं है।
> वसूली का नियम, बिना पैसे post करने की आम permission नहीं है।

The same wrong version is said twice before the part moves on. Variants of the bulk-cash, revenue/service-stamp and postcard traps also recur in the recaps. Merge the repeated distractors into one explanation and use correct facts in the recap.

**Fix:** Exam में फर्क याद रखिए: वसूली का नियम unpaid posting की आम छूट नहीं देता।

### Series continuity: short franking reminder
> Franked articles, authorised posting व्यवस्था से ही दिए जाते हैं।
> मतलब तय counter पर।
> Post office के letter box में नहीं, और mail van के letter box में भी नहीं।
> Letter box में डाला गया machine-franked article, unpaid माना जाता है।

The preceding lesson already taught where machine-franked articles are tendered and what happens if they go in a letter box. The topic merits a short reminder here, not a second explanation.

**Fix:** Machine-franked article तय counter पर दीजिए। Post Office या mail van के letter box में डाला, तो उसे unpaid माना जाएगा।

### Screen lists: put the complete facts in production
> एक, collectors को बिकने वाला material, जैसे first day covers, special covers और annual packs।
> दो, बनाने का material, जैसे artworks, proofs और printing plates, और postmarks और slogan dies।

The voice counts two groups, but the production instruction was too generic to prevent missing entries. The source also has concrete minimum letter-card dimensions absent from the production handoff.

**Fix:** दो groups हैं: collection वाला material और stamps बनाने व mark करने वाला material। First day covers और printing plates इनके दो examples हैं। Spell out the complete philatelic inventory and the folded/unfolded dimensions on their respective cards.

### Rule 7: state the scope of each failure clearly
> Regulation 7 चार हालात बताता है।
> इनमें stamp, franking impression या point-of-sale receipt से postage paid नहीं माना जाता।
> एक, Rules या Regulations में उसका ऐसा इस्तेमाल दिया ही नहीं गया।
> दो, stamp मिटा हुआ, बिगड़ा हुआ, फटा, कटा, या किसी और तरह अधूरा है।
> तीन, posting से पहले उस पर कोई word, letter, figure या design छापा गया, Director General के अलावा किसी और ने।
> चार, stamp को embossed envelope, postcard या wrapper से काटकर अलग किया गया।

The opening sentence implies that every subcondition automatically applies in exactly the same way to all three forms. Preserve the scope of the individual conditions on the card.

**Fix:** Regulation 7 में चार वजहें हैं, जिनसे भुगतान मान्य नहीं होता। पहली, Rules या Regulations उस तरीके को मान्यता नहीं देते। दूसरी, stamp मिटा हुआ है या खराब कर दिया गया है। तीसरी, posting से पहले stamp, franking impression या counter receipt पर किसी और ने कुछ छापा। ऐसी printing सिर्फ Director General कर सकते हैं। चौथी, stamp embossed envelope, postcard या wrapper से अलग किया गया।

### Booking: teach the cancellation conditions directly
> Cancellation का नियम ये वादा नहीं करता कि हर unused खरीद का पैसा लौटेगा।
> न ये कि dispatch के बाद हर booking का पैसा बिना शर्त मिलेगा।

The material does warn against assuming unconditional refunds after dispatch. State that boundary through the actual cancellation windows and refund condition, rather than a second negative version of the trap.

**Fix:** Cancellation पर refund की अपनी शर्तें हैं।

### Narration: teach the examples without referring to the material
> Material का example देखिए।

The voice should teach the calculation without referring to the source or package. The same issue occurs in the letter-card and postcard examples.

**Fix:** एक 35 grams का letter लीजिए।

### Postcard: six conditions belong on a card, not a readout
> Postcard की शर्तें।
> Postage पूरा prepaid।
> वज़न 5 grams से ज़्यादा नहीं।
> Size, 140 by 90 mm।
> बाकी तीन शर्तें card की बनावट की हैं।
> Address side पर Post Card लिखा या छपा हो, English या Hindi में।
> Card, Post Office के postcard से पतला या ज़्यादा लचीला न हो।
> और न मोड़ा जाए, न cover में रखा जाए, न काटा जाए, न किसी और तरह बदला जाए।
> हाँ, छपे stamp पर initials की perforation हो सकती है।

The full six-condition readout crowds the postcard chapter, already heavy with measurements and exceptions. Put every condition on the card and keep the voice on the most actionable details.

**Fix:** Screen पर postcard की छह शर्तें हैं: तीन payment, वज़न और size पर, तीन card की बनावट पर। Size ठीक 140 by 90 mm और वज़न अधिकतम 5 grams। Card खुला भेजिए। उसे मोड़िए नहीं, और cover में मत रखिए। छपे stamp पर initials के छोटे छेद लगाने की छूट है।

### Delivery: preserve the exact refusal instruction
> तब Postman cover पर Refused लिखता है।

The rule specifies that the marking goes across the cover, not just anywhere on it.

**Fix:** तब Postman cover की चौड़ाई में Refused लिखता है।

### Reproduction: distinguish the permitted form from the regulation's wording
> ऐसी reproduction black में होनी चाहिए।
> Regulation खुद कोई colour नहीं लिखता।
> Reproduction का form, administrative instructions से तय होता है।
> इसका मतलब ये नहीं कि stamp को किसी भी रूप में छापने की खुली छूट है।

The requirement to use black and the administrative control over permitted form can be taught directly. The distinction that the regulation itself does not name a colour belongs in the owner's notes.

**Fix:** Illustration काले रंग में छापिए। किस रूप में छाप सकते हैं, यह administrative instructions तय करती हैं।

### Closing recap: keep the exam distinctions, not the distractors
> Mail services का postage cash, digital payment, stamps या franking से दिया जाता है।

The closing line about payment omits the other prescribed means. The last point can also sound as though every previously used stamp triggers this consequence regardless of the stated fraudulent reuse or intent.

**Fix:** Mail services के चार मुख्य तरीके हैं: cash, digital, stamps और authorised franking। Regulations में बताया दूसरा तरीका भी मान्य है।

### Exact text versus gist on screen
> Document cards (Georgia, white, source beneath): Act 2023 section 4 stamp definition; postal stationery definition; Regulation 7's four conditions; Regulation 16 affixing; Regulation 23A cancellation (from 15 July 2026); Regulation 89 and 91 conditions. Hindi gist labelled "सार (gist)". Rule numbers not spoken (Rule 10, Regulations 6, 8, 13, 14, 16, 59, 60, 63, 90, 91) appear only on these cards.

The notes did not clearly distinguish official wording from the package's summaries. Nor did the package identify its Hindi translation as official.

**Fix:** Show verbatim legal text only where the supplied material gives it as such. Otherwise show a labelled सार and the provision/year; don't imply the Hindi translation is official.

## Passed

- Topic named first, no greeting, quiz, story hook, promotion or exam-name lead-in.
- The ten-part map, teaching order, numbering and earlier lessons agree; the one material overlap is handled above.
- The source examples are intact: embossed-envelope credit, the letter-card and postcard breach calculations, and all three domestic postage-due calculations.
- The specific effective dates remain attached to the relevant changes; no obsolete rule numbers, past-paper claims or invented tariffs were introduced.
- Cash facility thresholds, definitive versus commemorative use, sale permission and stock handling, booking cancellation, rates and the BNS penalties agree with the supplied material.
- Everyday Hinglish and a respectful tone predominate. Dense lists and a few longer legal sentences are the exceptions addressed above.
- No off-limits wording, misleading claims, service dispute or other-lecture references.
- Recaps after Parts 2, 4, 6 and 8; no chapter-sized departure from the material. Spoken references to provision numbers are not repeated.
- Contents and assumptions that need source confirmation stay in production notes; the ₹1 minimum/rounding are not imported into the letter-card breach.

## Weakest part

Parts 7–9 make the learner switch quickly between similar card rules, measurements and recovery calculations. The full specifications belong on separate, held cards; the voice should name the decisive distinction and then work one calculation at a time. The source's full coverage makes these dense sections; compliance with the three-new-facts-per-minute ceiling still depends on actual recording and card timings, not a text-only check.
