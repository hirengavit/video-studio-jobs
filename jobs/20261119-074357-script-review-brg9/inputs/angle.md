# Payment of postage, stamps and postal stationery: the plan

## The plan

**Promise.** Postage कैसे चुकाया जाता है, कौन-सा stamp या stationery valid गिना जाता है, और कम या बिना postage वाले item पर कितना वसूला जाता है: पूरा topic, current Rules और Regulations से, साफ क्रम में।

**Opening (R1).** Topic named plainly: payment of postage, stamps और postal stationery. One line of background: इसके नियम Post Office Act 2023, Rules 2024 और Regulations 2024 में हैं; Regulations 16 December 2024 से लागू। The whole picture in a few short lines (postage पहले देना, चार तरीके, stamp valid और सही जगह हो, stationery पर छपा postage credit, कमी पर दोगुना), then the map of 10 parts. No hook, no story, no exam names.

**Chapters, in the material's own order (big to small, idea → meaning → example → exam angle):**

1. **आज का topic और पूरी तस्वीर** — topic, background line, whole picture, map.
2. **Part 1 · Postage, prepayment और payment के तरीके** — postage and prepayment defined; stamp = evidence of payment; Act section 4: physical or digital, all forms; postal stationery defined; Post Office's exclusive privilege; DG regulations on supply and sale; Central Government or DG issue stamps; selling ≠ issuing; Regulation 5 prepayment mandatory unless the Regulations say otherwise; recovery rules are not a licence to post unpaid (trap); fully prepaid articles go by the earliest mail; BNPL as the authorised exception (writing "BNPL" does not create it); Rule 9's five modes for mail services; other postal services paid by the modes in the Regulations (don't mix the two).
3. **Part 2 · Cash, digital payment और franking** — cash and digital expressly allowed; amount must cover all charges; QR code = digital payment, dynamic QR under Advanced Postal Technology, transaction must record success; counter payment needs no stamps; payment mode vs evidence on the item; franking impression replaces stamps for its value, must be authorised, legible, unaltered; placement rules apply to franking; India Post Parcel: DG-approved vendors' machine models; franked articles at the designated counter, in a letter box treated as unpaid (short, as already taught in the series); bulk-cash facilities authorised by the Circle Head: 500 packets big cities, 250 small towns; 500 articles at selected Gazetted and HSG offices (letters, inland letter cards, postcards); trap: not a minimum for ordinary counter cash; India Post Parcels are accountable; from 1 October 2025 registration is a value-added service for Speed Post documents and parcels.
4. **Recap · Part 1 और Part 2** — four keywords.
5. **Part 3 · Stamps के प्रकार और philately** — five types on a table (definitive, commemorative, special, joint issue, My Stamp) with purpose and distinction; definitive from 25 paise at all offices; commemorative may pay postage (trap), is not a revenue stamp or charity label; philately defined (includes stationery and postmarks); Philatelic Bureaux and counters; philatelic material list on screen, in two groups; DG decides what is sold; overseas orders at the Indian Philatelic Bureau, Bombay GPO; accessories are not proof of payment; proposals by any citizen, examined under Central Government instructions, design and denomination approved by Central Government; no stamp on a living personality; mandatory purchase for institution/event proponents, relaxable; Philatelic Advisory Committee and its ex officio Chairperson (Union Minister in charge of Communications), who nominates members.
6. **Part 4 · Valid और invalid stamps, और stamp लगाने का तरीका** — printed value alone is not proof; Regulation 7's four conditions; the embossed cut-out example; identifying perforation (minute holes) as the exception, country and value legible, not a licence to cut embossed stamps; four look-alikes on a table (postage, revenue, service, charity/decorative) with the revenue-stamp sale point and the "only valid postage counts" rule, service stamps not for an employee's personal letter; affixing: top right-hand corner of the address side, completely adherent, not on contents or a tied label, also for franking; minimum practicable number and why; non-postal labels off the address side, not cancelled; invalid payment vs fraud (reuse → non-delivery).
7. **Recap · Part 3 और Part 4** — four keywords.
8. **Part 5 · बिक्री, stock, refund और booking cancellation** — DG's prior permission for anyone, including electronic platforms; contravention punishable; operational instructions; sale prices (denoted value; schedule prices for embossed and registration envelopes; Rakhi Envelopes by the Circle Head; philatelic items); custody rules (safe, wooden or tin box, protective case with blotting paper, most stock inside by day, a day's or half-day's need out, earlier supply first); unused stamps and stationery normally not refunded or exchanged, with why "normally" matters; Regulation 23A from 15 July 2026: self-booked items before acceptance, counter bookings same day, Post Office cancels wrong data entry same day, refund under administrative instructions; trap: returning stamps ≠ cancelling a booking.
9. **Part 6 · Postal stationery और उस पर छपा postage** — what makes stationery prepaid; postcard, letter card, embossed envelope, aerogramme (international, not an inland letter card); price table (₹0.50, ₹2.50, ₹5, ₹22.50, ₹23 with the ₹22 + stationery-charge split); stationery charge is not postage; buying the envelope is not a registered booking; shortfall made up with stamps: the material's 35-gram letter in a ₹5 embossed envelope; registration envelope's ₹22 credited; Rakhi Envelope not assumed to equal an embossed envelope.
10. **Recap · Part 5 और Part 6** — four keywords.
11. **Part 7 · Letter card** — definition, nothing enclosed or attached; private letter cards allowed if they meet the conditions, closed on 3 sides; Regulation 89's five conditions (prepaid in full, 5 g, 70 gsm, "Letter Card" at top left of the address side, nothing attached); folded vs unfolded maximums said, minimums and flaps on a table; flaps may be omitted; breach → treated as a letter, double the deficiency: the ₹2.50 → ₹5 example; extra stamps don't cure an enclosure; inland rate is not for foreign mail, aerogramme is different, unpaid outward international items returned without charge.
12. **Part 8 · Postcard** — definition; Regulation 91 conditions (prepaid, 5 g, 140 × 90 mm, then the three about the card itself, grouped); impressed stamp may carry initials perforation; right half vs left half and back; sender's address, stamp-position marks; the four permitted attachments and where the labels go; breach → letter, double the deficiency, ₹1 minimum, rounding up: the 50 paise → ₹9 example; postcard names (single, reply, printed, Meghdoot) with reply and printed explained; names are not separate tariffs; printed advertisements allowed.
13. **Recap · Part 7 और Part 8** — four keywords.
14. **Part 9 · Unpaid और कम postage वाले items** — unpaid, insufficiently paid, deficiency; only valid payment counts; Regulation 71 table (double the prepaid rate, double the deficiency, ₹1 minimum), charged on delivery or from the sender on return; the three worked calculations (₹10, ₹4, ₹1); trap: don't ignore valid postage; hold for general delivery, no extra security; Postmaster or officer in charge corrects three kinds of charging errors; postage-due stamp and initials, what it is not; delivery: pay the marked due or refuse, Postman writes "Refused"; refused and unclaimed items go straight back to the office of posting, else the Returned Letter Office; recovery from the sender; Circle Head may remit postage on a maliciously unpaid item, not automatic; outward international items returned without charge.
15. **Part 10 · Stamp का दोबारा इस्तेमाल और fictitious stamps** — fraudulent reuse banned, faint or removed cancellation doesn't help; detected on a posted item → not delivered, further action under instructions, collecting a deficiency is not enough; fictitious stamp defined; forged, counterfeit, fictitious punishable; BNS from 1 July 2024, three offences on a table (2 years, 3 years, ₹200 fine), seizure and forfeiture; trap: ₹200 belongs only to the fictitious-stamp offence, and fictitious ≠ reuse; reproduction for philatelic illustration, in black, form set by administrative instructions, no open licence.
16. **याद रखने वाली बातें** — seven plain points, the material's key points first, each confusion said as the correct fact.
17. **Close** — one warm line, no mention of other lectures.

**How it ends.** The recap in plain teacher's sentences, then one warm line about the counter.
