# Affixing stamps, date stamps and bulk cash prepayment

## The plan

**Promise.** After this lecture the viewer knows where and how postage is shown on an item (stamp position, the 40 mm top gap, date stamps of at least 2 offices), where each kind of item may be posted, and the three different payment permissions: bulk cash prepayment (Head of the Circle), a newspaper's licence to post without prepayment (Head of the Circle or Region) and the Business Reply permit (Head of the Division), with every number that goes with them.

**Opening (R1).** The topic named plainly; one line of background (since 16 December 2024 these rules sit in the Post Office Regulations 2024, before that in Post Office Guide Part I, whose clause numbers old papers still ask); the whole idea in two questions: where and how postage shows on an item, and when, how and with whose permission it is paid. Then the map of seven parts. No hook, story or running example.

**Chapters, in the material's own order (big to small; each idea before its trap):**

1. **Topic and big picture.**
2. **Map.** Seven parts, numbered, named after their regulation or clause.
3. **Part 1 · Regulation 16: placing the stamp.** Postage and prepayment defined; prepayment mandatory unless an authorised exception; the payment modes; counter cash vs a bulk cash permission. Old vs new: Guide clause 9 "desirable", unpaid items no extra security, could be held, no special deliveries → mandatory from 16 December 2024. Right-hand top corner of the address side; whole stamp stuck to the item itself, not contents, label or tied tag (trap: tag). Same rule for franking. Higher denominations, no fixed maximum. Embossed postage topped up only by the difference (₹5 + ₹3 = ₹8 example, assumed figures). Revenue and charity stamps don't pay postage, aren't cancelled; no confusing marks on the address side. Kept short where it repeats earlier series facts (R14), but every point said.
4. **Part 2 · Regulation 18: space on the envelope, and date stamps.** The four measures (40 mm top gap to the address block, 15 mm edges, 10 mm clear zone, stamp within the 74 mm maximum area at upper right); trap: 40 mm is not the stamp's position. Old vs new: 3 cm (Guide clause 26) → 40 mm; which to write. Postage stamp = payment, date stamp = dated handling. At least 2 offices (booking or posting, destination); "at least"; trap: not 2 adhesive stamps. Strong cover; wax-cloth inside, strong paper outside.
5. **Part 3 · Regulation 22: where and how to post.** Letter box list (8 classes, table on screen) only when fully prepaid; exempt items. Old vs new: small packets removed from 1 January 2026 (Sixth Amendment Regulations 2025). Registered newspapers, periodical post and franked items not in letter boxes → treated as unpaid (double rate, double deficiency, minimum ₹1; from addressee or returned sender). Accountable items at the counter (what accountable means); a large item taken by hand is not thereby accountable. Bulk bundle at the window: postage and late fee prepaid. Franchisee outlet, self-booking kiosk (Regional Head designates offices), web or app. Franked mail: designated office, date of franking with statement of mailing, or very next working day; relaxation in writing by the licensing authority (Divisional Head or independent Gazetted Postmaster), not the bulk cash authority; trap.
6. **First recap card.** Four keywords from Parts 1–3.
7. **Part 4 · Guide clause 12: bulk cash prepayment.** What it is and why; Head of the Circle authorises the offices; not every office. The three arrangements (500 big cities, 250 small towns, 500 at selected Gazetted and HSG offices); 300-packet example; quantity alone is not enough; trap: 500 is not a minimum for every cash payment. Old vs new: unregistered parcels → all India Post Parcels accountable, later collection only with DG's permission. The earlier 50-article facility (five cities, now Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai; special registered journals; same type and weight; 50 is a count, not grams) → registration a value-added service for Speed Post from 1 October 2025. Cash not a universal concession; similar procedure for registered newspapers; today rule 9 plus each product's conditions.
8. **Part 5 · Regulation 95: newspapers posted without prepayment.** Pay after, vs bulk cash pay before. Separate licence besides postal registration; Head of the Circle or Head of the Region; more than 500 copies (500 fails, 501 passes; vs bulk cash "at least 500"). WPP before the licence number. Invoice in duplicate; Form MS-98 fortnightly bills on the 16th and the 1st; copies to HO and Postal Accounts Office; credited to "Postage realised in cash for ordinary services". Pay within 7 days of receiving the bill; default, deemed cancellation, security deposit, balance under the 2023 Act; licence ends with postal registration, renew both together. Not a general unstamped permission; the Business Reply permit is a different thing. Trap: not postage-free.
9. **Second recap card.** Four keywords from Parts 4–5.
10. **Part 6 · Business Reply Service: who pays, and the permit.** How it works; respondent posts without a stamp; permit holder is the addressee and pays usual postage + ₹1 handling per article (separate from the permit fee and from unpaid-letter charges). Old vs new: 10 paise → ₹1 from 1 February 2002. Application with printed proofs to the Postmaster of the return office → Head of the Division issues; fee in advance. Quarterly fees ₹200, ₹150, ₹100, ₹50; financial year; expiry 31 March; renewal during March from 1 April, ₹200, no grace period; default cancels. October 2026 example. Trap: the fee doesn't cover the replies' postage.
11. **Part 7 · Preparing Business Reply articles, and the advance deposit.** An ordinary envelope with a firm's address isn't one. Six particulars on the address side (table on screen; voice gives three groups and names the two lines and the two indications); space for postmarks; label design in advertisements. Language rules. Card not folded, cut or altered; double postcard or enclosed; envelope must be enclosed. Sizes (140 by 90 mm, ±2 mm; postcard 140 by 90, not thinner or more flexible); old Guide link. Address side: only the Business Reply Label; communication side: sender label up to 5 by 2 cm, thin-paper pictures. Breach → unpaid letter. Advance deposit about 15 days; daily account; replenish before the fortnight from the 1st or 16th or on exhaustion, whichever is earlier; the permit fee is no substitute. Then the three permissions side by side (the material's closing trap).
12. **Guide clauses and today's rules.** Guide replaced on 16 December 2024; clause map on screen (9 → Reg 5, 12 → Rule 9, 15 → none, 20 → Reg 22, 23 → Reg 16, 24 → Reg 25, 25 → none, 26 → Reg 18, 116–118 → none); voice names the pairs exams turn on; clauses 15 to 25 as the general packing and posting rules; clause 24 and franking-like impressions; UCR for the permit fee in older answer keys.
13. **याद रखने वाली बातें.** Five plain sentences, key points first, only correct facts.
14. **Close.** One warm line.

**Ending.** The recap ticks the five points on the envelope card; one warm line about looking at the top-right corner of the next envelope. No mention of other lectures.
