## Problems

### Opening promise and map
> Where postage goes on an item, where each item is posted, and the three ways a sender can pay without a stamp on every article: bulk cash, a newspaper licence and Business Reply.
Business Reply is paid by the returning reply’s addressee, not by the person posting the reply. The map also merges Parts 6 and 7 although their contents and labels differ.
**Fix:** State that bulk cash is paid before posting, licensed newspaper postage after billing, and a Business Reply by its addressee. Give Part 6 (permit and charges) and Part 7 (article conditions and advance deposit) separate map lines.

### Series overlap and source coverage, Part 1
> किसी अलग label पर नहीं, और item से बँधी या जुड़ी किसी चीज़ पर भी नहीं।
The rule is right, but the section teaches the tag trap three times and substantially repeats payment and validity already taught. The core placement, exceptions, and balance example all still belong here.
**Fix:** Keep a compact Rule 9 and postage-due recap, one tied-tag example and one line for contents and separate labels. Keep the embossed ₹5/₹8/₹3 example. Keep the old unpaid-mail conditions on the comparison card instead of reteaching all three in voice.

### Date stamps, exact rule language and production geometry
> और इसका मतलब ये भी नहीं कि sender को 2 postage stamps लगाने हैं।
The very next line repeats the same wrong two-adhesive-stamps option. The graphics also treat “maximum area of 74 mm” like a fully specified area although the reading gives length units.
**Fix:** Say once that the count is office postmarks, then repeat only the correct booking/destination rule. Keep the exact supplied 74 mm wording on the sourced gist card and flag the unexplained units/geometry to the owner.

### Posting charges and definition, Part 3
> कम paid item पर कमी का दोगुना, और कम से कम ₹1।
This puts the ₹1 minimum only in the sentence about underpaid items, making it sound like the minimum does not also apply to unpaid domestic mail. The accountable-item passage starts with the legal term instead of the job the receipt and signature do.
**Fix:** Put the minimum on a separate line after both calculations, then distinguish collection from the addressee or returned sender. Say receipt and proof of delivery first, then name the category.

### Clause 12 thresholds and series context, Part 4
> Option कहे कि हर cash payment या हर service का minimum 500 है, तो वो गलत है।
The negative option repeats a distinction already made and does not resolve the earlier lesson calling 500 and 250 old Guide figures. Reading five city names and their three renames adds needless density.
**Fix:** Say these figures belong to the named Guide arrangements, while ordinary counter cash has no such general minimum. Name the historic five-city arrangement, journal, count and identical type/weight in voice; put all city names and renames on screen.

### Newspaper qualification and boundary, Part 5
> शर्त ये है कि newspaper उन्हीं के क्षेत्र में छपता हो।
The supplied English qualification is where a newspaper is published, not where it is printed. The 500 contrast is stated four ways, and the WPP instruction is hard to parse in one line.
**Fix:** Say “उसी क्षेत्र से प्रकाशित होता हो।” Teach 500 versus 501 once, without treating count as sufficient for a licence. Print the licence number below postal registration and WPP immediately before the licence number.

### Business Reply traps and long list, Parts 6–7
> और दो lines: No postage stamp necessary, if posted in India।
The voice reads virtually the whole six-item legend after promising a table, and reads both official indications verbatim. The outdated 10-paise figure is also unnecessarily repeated.
**Fix:** Narrate the three categories, the two vertical marks and who pays; show all six entries and both quoted indications on the card. Give the 10-paise historical figure once and the current ₹1 in its correct context.

### Prepaid-rate reference after Business Reply breach
> तब unpaid वाला दोगुना charge लगता है।
The blanket “double charge” here drops the earlier distinction between an unpaid rate and a deficiency, the ₹1 floor, and the contrast with the handling charge for compliant replies.
**Fix:** Cross-reference the Part 3 domestic rules, including the minimum, and state positively that a compliant reply costs the permit holder usual postage plus ₹1 handling.

### Recap, time density and historical cards
> Newspaper licence 500 से ज़्यादा copies पर मिलता है, और Business Reply में addressee हर जवाब पर postage और ₹1 देता है।
The closing point misses the distinct authorities, the newspaper payment deadline, and the Business Reply deposit and renewal boundaries despite their exam value. Two recap cards alone do not establish a seven-minute maximum or three-fact pacing.
**Fix:** Keep five grouped closing points; put the authorities and deadlines back. Add intra-lesson recap and card-hold cues, and the dated Guide-to-Regulations card; confirm pacing against the actual voice track, which is not yet timed.

## Weakest part

The switch from Part 5 billing and default to Parts 6–7 has the highest listening load. It puts three permissions, several dates, two different quarterly/fortnightly cycles and a six-item card close together. Use one comparison graphic (who authorises, who pays, and when) followed by staggered fee, expiry, and deposit cards. Confirm pace in an actual voice read; the Markdown checker does not measure seconds or on-screen dwell.
