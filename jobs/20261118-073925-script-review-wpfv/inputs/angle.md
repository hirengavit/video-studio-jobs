# Department of Posts structure, circles and their headquarters — lesson plan

## The plan

**Promise:** By the end, the viewer can place every post in the Department, from the Minister down to the Sub-division. They can tell look-alike posts and Boards apart, match all 23 civil Circles with their headquarters and their territorial exceptions, and know the Base Circle, the specialised offices and the training institutions.

**Opening (R1, no hook):** Name the topic plainly. Give one line of background: India Post is part of the Ministry of Communications, and its roots go back to 1 October 1854. Then the whole picture in two sentences: Ministry → Department (Secretary, DGPS) → Postal Services Board → 23 civil Circles → Regions, Divisions, Sub-divisions, plus one Base Circle for the Army. Then the map of eight parts.

**Chapters, in the material's own order (big to small):**

1. **Topic and the whole structure.** The topic, India Post in the Ministry of Communications, 1 October 1854, and the top-to-bottom picture in a few lines.
2. **The map.** The eight parts, numbered. The section labels use these names.
3. **Part 1: The Department and its central administration.** What India Post provides. The Minister of Communications, the Minister of State, and Rafi Ahmed Kidwai as the first Communications Minister. Secretary (Posts) heads the Department and chairs the PSB. Dak Bhawan, Sansad Marg, New Delhi 110001. The Postal Directorate and its "D" Section working as the Central Bag Office. Secretary vs DGPS: policy vs operations, with the duties revised on 27 March 2026 (so not every administrative matter belongs to the DG). Appointments: the President appoints Secretary (Posts); the Central Government appoints the DG (Act 2023 definition, plus an authorised officer). The DG's power to make regulations, and to introduce, modify or discontinue products and enter collaborations (Rules 2024, rule 4). The wording trap: "Head of the Department" and "Chairperson of the PSB" both mean Secretary (Posts), while "head of postal operations" means DGPS. The legal framework: Act 2023 (passing, assent and commencement dates; it repealed the 1898 Act), Rules 2024 (16 Dec 2024, superseding the 1933 Rules and keeping past actions valid), Regulations 2024 (16 Dec 2024), and later orders. History: Lord Dalhousie, stamps, RMS 1854, APS 1856, Postal Manual 1860. The vision. Key fact said twice: Secretary heads, DGPS runs operations.
4. **Part 2: The Postal Services Board.** Apex level policy-making body (rule 5), also called the apex management body. Perpetual succession and the common seal in plain words. The Central Government may specify its composition, powers and duties by Gazette notification (rule 6), and the apex status and the composition power are separate provisions. 1 Chairperson and 7 Members, with Secretary (Posts) as ex officio Chairperson. The seven Members go on a screen table; the voice names Member (Operations) and Member (HRD). Portfolio names can change. The renaming of ADG (Coordination) as Member (Coordination) on 20 Sep 2024. The Financial Adviser as permanent invitee, listed as JS&FA, and the AS&FA trap (one combined post, not two invitees); the FA also heads the Internal Finance Wing. Secretary (PSB) vs Secretary (Posts). DDGs, Directors and ADGs, and the point that a DDG is not the DGPS.
5. **Recap card 1:** Ministry of Communications, Secretary (Posts), DGPS, Dak Bhawan, PSB 1 + 7.
6. **Part 3: Circles, Regions, Divisions, Sub-divisions.** 23 civil Circles under a CPMG. Each unit and its head (screen table), plus the RMS Sub-division (IRM or ASRM). The Rules' definitions of Circle Head, Regional Head, Regional Director and Divisional Head (which is wider than SPOs or SSPOs). The Regulations' meanings of "Postmaster General", "Superintendent" and "Director (Postal Services)". Postal vs RMS Divisions: what RMS means and covers (rail, road, river, air), and the SPOs/SSPOs vs SRM/SSRM trap. The 2026 common structure: 1 designated Head of Region at PMG or Director level (JAG or SAG); the circle Head of Logistics (PMG or Director (Logistics)), who reports to the Head of Circle, is reviewed by Member (Operations), controls RMS, FPOs, MMS and hubs, and has the Heads of Sales and Transportation reporting to them. Postal Divisions remain the main field unit. Implementation letter 15 Sep 2026, deadline 30 Sep 2026. Control vs appointment: the powers of 17 Feb 2025, and the Head of Sub-division as engaging authority only for GDS ABPM and Dak Sevak posts assigned to them. The CPMG fixes the jurisdiction and HQ of Divisions and Sub-divisions with the Circle IFA. The 23 Postal Accounts Offices (SAG or JAG) act as IFA to the CPMG. The network numbers go on a screen table.
7. **Part 4: The 23 Circles and their headquarters.** What "headquarters" means, and that it need not be the state capital. The full list on a screen table, never read aloud. The voice teaches the pairs: Gujarat–Ahmedabad; Haryana–Ambala Cantt vs Punjab–Chandigarh; AP–Vijayawada vs Telangana–Hyderabad; Assam–Guwahati vs North East–Shillong; Karnataka–Bengaluru (Mysuru is training and technology); Kerala–Thiruvananthapuram. J&K: Srinagar, with the Circle Office also at Jammu, still one Circle. Older names: Bangalore, Trivandrum, Orissa.
8. **Recap card 2:** 23 civil Circles, CPMG, SSPO vs SSRM, Head of Logistics, the key HQ pairs.
9. **Part 5: Circle jurisdictions and exceptions.** Jurisdiction and "co-terminus" in plain words. The nine-row exceptions table on screen. Gujarat Circle covers DNH and DD (merged 26 Jan 2020), and Daman is not in Maharashtra Circle just because Goa is. North East Circle has 6 states; Assam has its own Circle and Sikkim is with West Bengal. Puducherry: Yanam goes to AP, Mahe to Kerala, the rest to Tamil Nadu; the general word Puducherry or Pondicherry means Tamil Nadu. A&N goes to WB; Lakshadweep (Laccadive Islands) to Kerala; Goa to Maharashtra; Chandigarh to Punjab. Postal arrangements don't change constitutional status. The material's Daman employee example.
10. **Part 6: The Base Circle and the Army Postal Service.** APS is organised as the Base Circle: 23 civil Circles plus 1 Base Circle. Its head is the ADG APS, a Major General. The APS Directorate is at Sena Dak Bhawan, New Delhi (1856), and the Defence Minister is responsible for APS. Officers come on deputation (explained); about 75% of other ranks come from the DoP and 25% are recruited by the Army, and the figure is for other ranks only. The 2 CBPOs: 1 CBPO, 56 APO, New Delhi, 900056; 2 CBPO, 99 APO, Kolkata, 900099; and what areas each APO serves. APO and FPO. Army PINs start with 9. The 2 CBPOs are not the whole FPO network.
11. **Recap card 3:** jurisdiction exceptions, NE Circle 6 states, Base Circle, 900056 and 900099.
12. **Part 7: Specialised offices, technology, training and the PO Guide.** A territorial unit vs a specialised office. PLI Directorate: New Delhi, Chanakyapuri, 1884 (Director PLI Kolkata is a different office). Parcel Directorate: New Delhi, Malcha Marg, 2018, headed by a CGM. Business Development Directorate (1996, Dak Bhawan) is a former designation; the CCS & RB and Parcel Directorates are named separately. Secretary (PSB) became HoD of the CCS & RB Directorate from 19 Aug 2026: it is treated as a division of the Postal Directorate, with no separate budget, and its reporting is unchanged. The Head of Logistics does not mean a national Logistics Directorate. PSD, CSD, PSD/CSD under a Superintendent, and the Group B officer in charge. MMS. Foreign Post and the Controller of Foreign Mails, Mumbai. CEPT: Mysuru, Head at Bengaluru, other offices, the Disaster Recovery Centre (15 May 2015) and the Post Info app. RAKNPA at Ghaziabad. The 6 PTCs on screen, with the voice naming Vadodara for Gujarat. The 7th PTC's foundation stone at Shivpuri (2 Sep 2026), not yet working. The CEPT vs PTC trap at Mysuru. The PO Guide's 4 parts; Part I has 7 sections and begins with clause 1, Control; the Guide applies where it is consistent with the Act, Rules and Regulations.
13. **Part 8: Other Boards, and the organisation at a glance.** "Board" is not always the PSB. The five-Board table on screen. PSSWB is chaired by the Minister, with Secretary (Posts) as Executive Vice-Chairman. Postal Sports Board: Secretary chairs, DGPS is Co-Chairperson, Member (HRD) is Vice-Chairperson. The Circle Welfare Board is chaired by the CPMG and the Circle Sports Board by the Head of Circle. The seven distinctions, said once each in short lines.
14. **याद रखने वाली बातें.** Six short correct-fact points, in the order of the material's key points.
15. **Close.** One warm line.

**Ending:** The plain recap of the points that matter most, then one warm line. No mention of other lectures.
