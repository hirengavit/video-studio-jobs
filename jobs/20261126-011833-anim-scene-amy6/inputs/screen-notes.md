- **For the owner to check:** this lecture teaches the general poste restante holding period as 15 days (the material's figure). The COD and VP lecture said: "Poste Restante पते वाला VP article, ज़्यादा से ज़्यादा 7 दिन रुकता है। बाकी Poste Restante डाक को मिलने वाला, 1 महीने तक का समय, इसे नहीं मिलता।" The VP 7 days fits this lecture's line that a COD service's own instruction sets its special period, but "1 महीने" and "15 दिन" cannot both be the general period. Please check which is current; if it is 15 days, the COD lecture's line needs changing, and if it is 1 month, this lecture's Part 2, recap cards and timeline card do.
