# Letter boxes and duties of the letter-box peon — plan

## The plan

**Promise.** After this lecture the viewer knows what may and may not go into a letter box, and the letter-box peon's whole job: the three times to know, the hour plate, the round with keys and bag, the electronic record, what to do with money or stamps found in a box, and how the Postmaster checks the work.

**Opening (R1).** Name the topic plainly: letter boxes and the duties of the letter-box peon. One line on what a letter box is (for posting fully prepaid mail, cleared by a postal employee), what clearance means, and that the Postal Manual calls the peon the letter-box attendant. The whole picture in two lines: posting rules decide what goes in; the attendant's rules decide when and how it comes out, and under whose check. Then the map of seven parts.

**Building on the series (R14).** The posting rules (the letter-box list, small packets, special boxes, accountable items, the three "unpaid" articles, double charge, the big article at the window, parcels, other modes of posting) and the Sunday and holiday arrangements were taught in full earlier. Here they get a short, plain recap with every fact of the material said once, without mentioning any other lecture. The new material, the attendant's duties, gets the most time.

**Chapters, in order:**

1. **Topic and big picture** — letter box, clearance, peon = letter-box attendant; the seven-part map.
2. **Part 1 · Letter box में कौन सी डाक** — a permitted mode of posting; where boxes are (POs, mail offices, public places, Mobile POs, steamers); the list in five groups on screen, postage fully prepaid; full prepayment alone isn't enough; small packets out. Trap: letter box (posting) vs rented post box (receiving). "For letters only" = letters and postcards (book packets out). Airmail and QMS boxes: only that class; others liable to detention (held up, not auto-returned or confiscated); QMS: full PIN below the town name.
3. **Part 2 · Box में नहीं, counter पर** — accountable item defined (receipt, signature ink or digital); counter presentation, receipt to the presenter; "Registered" written, extra stamps or box posting don't complete acceptance (trap: full postage ≠ proper booking). Domestic registration = value-added service on Speed Post documents and parcels, addressee-specific, still accountable; not the international registered letter. All India Post Parcels accountable (Regulations effective 16 December 2024). Franked, registered newspaper and periodical post items: box = unpaid; franking impression ≠ permission; rule applies every day, not just Sundays. Charges: unpaid = double prepaid rate; short-paid = double the deficiency; minimum ₹1; recovered on delivery or from sender on return; attendant neither fixes nor collects it. Big ordinary article at the window: not accountable by handover. Other modes: franchisee outlet, self-booking kiosk (Regional Head, not Circle Head; inaugurated World Post Day, 9 October 2023), web portal or app.
4. **Recap · Part 1 और Part 2** — envelope card.
5. **Part 3 · Attendant के समय और hour plate** — rule 116: three times (clearance, dispatch, mails arriving); why they connect; punctual clearance. Changeable hour plate shows the next clearance; the material's own 10:00 / 14:00 illustration; trap: not the clearance just done. Lock the box, secure plate and lock; an electronic entry doesn't replace the physical work.
6. **Part 4 · चाबी, bag और collection का चक्कर** — rule 117: Postmaster or responsible Assistant hands over keys, hour plates (where used) and a bag; custody stays with them; issued only at the fixed leaving hours; not kept between rounds (issue vs custody). The round's steps (on screen). Return without delay; hand back, received by the Postmaster or responsible attendant. Report damage or defects to the Postmaster. Sundays and holidays: closed except Night POs, no street-box clearance unless authorised; posting ≠ same-day clearance; late-fee boxes at RMS offices, selected offices, RMS mail vans; registered newspapers without late fee at PSO, RMS, NPO (acceptance, not a letter-box permission); machine-franked still out; Circle Head or Regional Head may specify Sunday and holiday opening and transactions.
7. **Recap · Part 3 और Part 4**.
8. **Part 5 · Clearance का electronic record** — what the record shows; Doorstep Service app for regular clearance; the Manual's Nanyatha procedure; exam angle: Nanyatha = letter-box clearance, not parcel booking or post-box allotment. The six steps (on screen, said in three groups). Why the barcode is inside the door; trap: the barcode doesn't count letters. GPS / GPRS / MIS. Stored coordinates and due time compared. Physical work and record must agree.
9. **Part 6 · Clearance में मिले पैसे, stamps और कीमती चीज़ें** — rule 154: hand to the Postmaster; four cases (loose stamps; unused private envelope or postcard with stamps; departmental postcard, embossed envelope, stamp booklet or sheet, currency note or current coin; other coins or valuables). Exam angle in three pairs.
10. **Recap · Part 5 और Part 6**.
11. **Part 7 · Postmaster की जाँच और attendant के extra काम** — the Postmaster's checks; test letter addressed to himself, result in the order book; trap: order book (test) vs Error Book (irregular posting). Head Postman may test the attendants' work. Rule 118: carrying mails to attached town SOs or BOs; packer work (stamping, facing, packing, closing and sealing bags); punctual clearance still comes first. Irregular posting isn't booking; bring it into the office. COD: what it is, agreement with the postal authority, accountable, never in a letter box. The four groups that tie the topic together.
12. **याद रखने वाली बातें** — five plain points, key points first.
13. **Close** — one warm line.

**Ending.** The recap of five points, then one warm line about the hour plate on the next letter box the viewer sees. No mention of other lectures.
