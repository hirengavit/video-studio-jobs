# Letter boxes and letter-box peon duties · Script review

## Problems

### Series continuity
> Box में कौन सी डाक जा सकती है, उसकी list screen पर है।
> इसमें पाँच groups हैं।
> Letters और letter cards, aerogrammes, और postcards के साथ airmail postcards।
> फिर book post और printed papers, और literature for the blind।
The earlier packing lesson teaches this already; the screen keeps the complete official list, including regular and airmail postcards, book post and printed papers.
**Fix:** Screen पर पाँच groups हैं: letters और letter cards; aerogrammes; postcards; printed mail; और literature for the blind।

### Repeated trap
> Plate पर दिखा समय, अभी पूरी हुई clearance का record नहीं है।
> Option कहे कि plate पिछली clearance का समय दिखाती है, तो गलत।
> Hour plate हमेशा आने वाली clearance का समय बताती है।
The example already shows the difference. Keep the positive rule once after it.
**Fix:** यानी plate अगली clearance बताती है।

### Sunday and holiday exceptions
> कुछ सुविधाएँ इन दिनों भी, तय घंटों में मिलती हैं।
Guide-derived arrangements are not blanket entitlements; notified openings and transactions under regulation 4(2) control.
**Fix:** जहाँ सुविधा authorised हो, वहाँ तय घंटों में ये arrangements लागू होते हैं।

### Equipment custody in the midpoint recap
> इनकी custody, Postmaster या responsible Assistant की।
The antecedent includes the bag, but the specific custody clause of Manual rule 117(1) names only keys and plates.
**Fix:** चाबियाँ और plates, Postmaster या responsible Assistant की custody में रहती हैं।

### Equipment custody in the closing recap
> चाबी, plates और bag, Postmaster या responsible Assistant की custody में रहते हैं।
> सिर्फ round के लिए मिलते हैं, और आखिरी box के बाद बिना देर लौटकर वापस।
Separate custody from issue and return, and retain the tested defect-reporting duty in the closing recap.
**Fix:** चाबी और plates, Postmaster या responsible Assistant की custody में रहती हैं।
Round के लिए bag भी मिलता है।
आखिरी box के बाद बिना देर लौटिए और तीनों वापस दीजिए।
Box में defect दिखे, तो Postmaster को बताइए।

### Current practice versus the Manual
> Nanyatha, letter box की clearance का software है।
The closing recap loses the distinction correctly introduced earlier. The official release identifies Doorstep Service use, not a date or nationwide withdrawal of Nanyatha.
**Fix:** Regular clearance के लिए Doorstep Service app इस्तेमाल होता है।
Manual की Nanyatha प्रक्रिया में box खुलता है, फिर scan और count दर्ज होते हैं।

### Resolved source query
> - For the owner to check: the material says the keys, plates and bag are issued by the "Postmaster or responsible Assistant" but received on return by the "Postmaster or responsible attendant". The script teaches it as given; please confirm whether "attendant" on return is intended or should read "Assistant".
The official Manual uses both terms for different steps. There is no factual question to leave for the owner.
**Fix:** - Rule 117(1) card: issue, Postmaster or responsible Assistant; custody of keys and plates, same; receipt of keys, plates and bag on return, Postmaster or responsible attendant. Verified in [India Post Postal Manual Vol. VI Part III](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/api/documents/file/postal-manual-vol-vi-part-iii-20250927-34c6b1), pp. 60–61. [PIB’s Doorstep Service reference](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2279315&lang=2&reg=48) does not specify a switch date or nationwide withdrawal.

### Size versus accountability
> अब उल्टा case।
> एक ordinary article box के लायक है, पर box के मुँह से अंदर नहीं जाता।
> उसे post office की window पर हाथ में दिया जा सकता है।
> पर हाथ में देने भर से, वो accountable नहीं बनता।
> Accountable उसे वो service बनाती है, जिसमें वो accept हुआ।
> Size और accountability, दो अलग सवाल हैं।
The earlier packing lesson covered this; preserve the example, not the prolonged repetition.

हाथ में देने से उसकी service नहीं बदलती; article ordinary ही रहता है।

## Passed

- Opening and map: topic, clearance, the attendant and the seven-part sequence are clear.
- Order and exam relevance: start with eligible mail, then exceptions, timing and custody, electronic clearance, finds and supervision. The 10:00 → 14:00 example is explicitly illustrative.
- Fact and coverage sweep against the English material: ordinary and special boxes, proper acceptance, tariff distinctions, opening arrangements, the full round, electronic checks, all four rule 154 cases, extra duties, COD and other posting modes are retained. Source and earlier videos were compared where topics overlap.
- Language and delivery: understandable Hindi in Devanagari with postal terms in English; sampled speech remains mostly short and one idea at a time. Neutral, respectful tone; no cadre dispute, exam-history claim, MCQ, sales pitch or off-limits line.
- Pacing: recaps after Parts 2, 4 and 6, with five closing points. Production should hold tables for reading rather than accelerating the list. Exact spoken pacing and the new-facts-per-minute check need a timed narration; no audio came with this job.
- Spoken rule numbers are not repeated, superseded parcel treatment is not taught and the approved existing references are kept in Production notes.

## Weakest part

The start of Part 4’s Sunday exceptions is the densest stretch of recycled material. Treat it as one comparison card under the rule about notified hours, with distinct rows for late-fee posting and newspaper acceptance. Leave the attendant’s actual round as the main lesson.
