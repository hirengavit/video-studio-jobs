- **Worked examples as calculation cards (Georgia on white):** TD ₹10,000 at 7.5%: 7.5 ÷ 4 = 1.875% a quarter; 10,000 × [(1 + 7.5/400)^4 − 1] ≈ ₹771.36 → ₹771; beside it "7.5% × 10,000 = ₹750" in slate with a strike-through.
