# Production notes (from the script)

- **Frame and recurring picture.** Section label top-left in English (e.g. "PART 1 · LITERATURE FOR THE BLIND", "PART 4 · GYAN POST", "PART 8 · BREACH AND CHOICE"), series pill top-right "POSTAL PRODUCTS". Recurring pictures, simple ink icons: an open book with raised Braille dots for Parts 1–3, a closed book with a "Gyan Post" tag for Parts 4–6, both side by side in Part 8. Key-term stamps (English bold, Hindi beneath): Literature for the Blind / नेत्रहीनों के लिए साहित्य; Braille / ब्रेल लिपि; second-class mail / द्वितीय श्रेणी की डाक; airmail fee / हवाई डाक शुल्क; Cecogrammes / नेत्रहीनों का साहित्य (विदेश डाक); proof of delivery (POD) / वितरण का प्रमाण; attested copy / अनुप्रमाणित प्रति; India Post Parcel – Retail / भारतीय डाक पार्सल – खुदरा; Detailed Transaction Report / विस्तृत लेन-देन रिपोर्ट; Bag Manifest / बैग की प्रेषण सूची. Recaps on the envelope card, each keyword ticking green as said (four recap cards: Parts 1–2, Part 3, Parts 4–5, Parts 6–7).
- **Map (chapter 1), one item per spoken line:** 1 Literature for the Blind में क्या जाता है · 2 Packet: नाम, packing, वज़न, size · 3 Postage, हवाई डाक, शर्त टूटना, विदेश डाक · 4 Gyan Post और योग्य किताबें · 5 अंदर, बाहर, packing और size · 6 Tariff · 7 POD, attested copy, registration, insurance · 8 वसूली और सही service. Split over two screens of four.
- **Gist cards (Georgia on white, source beneath, labelled "सार (gist)" because the material gives no exact regulation wording; no quote marks; voice never reads them):** Part 1 — Papers, periodicals and books impressed in Braille or another special type for the blind · Post Office Regulations 2024, Reg. 93(1). Part 4 — Reg. 92A inserted by the Post Office (Second Amendment) Regulations 2025, G.S.R. 263(E), notified 28 April 2025; in force 1 May 2025; made by the Director General under the Post Office Act 2023 with prior Central Government approval. Part 7 — No fee is charged for an attested copy for a blind-literature item · Reg. 136(4); registration is a value-added service for Speed Post documents and parcels, with addressee-specific delivery · Post Office (Fourth Amendment) Regulations 2025, from 1 October 2025. Each with a Hindi सार line.
- **Tables and lists handed to the screen (write exactly these):**
  - *Blind literature size (Part 2):* Roll: लंबाई — 100 mm से 800 mm · Roll: लंबाई + 2 × diameter — 170 mm से 1000 mm · Roll के अलावा — minimum 100 × 70 mm, maximum 600 × 300 × 300 mm. Then the roll sum building line by line: 700 + (2 × 160) = 700 + 320 = 1020 mm > 1000 mm ✗ (slate cross on 1020).
  - *Four checks before acceptance (Part 2):* 1 सामान योग्य; आम छपाई या साथ की चीज़ छूट के अंदर · 2 बाहर "Literature for the Blind" + sender का नाम-पता · 3 cover नहीं, या दोनों सिरों से खुला, आसानी से हटने वाला · 4 वज़न और हर लागू size सीमा में.
  - *Airmail sum (Part 3):* 125 g = 50 g (₹2) + 50 g (₹1) + 25 g (₹1) → ₹4; postage ₹0.
  - *Cecogrammes card (Part 3):* label "Cecogrammes", सामने ऊपर दाहिने कोने में · sender का पता ज़रूरी · Braille या खास लिपि में letters, पत्रिकाएँ, किताबें, plates · sound recordings सिर्फ recognised संस्था से या उसे · stamps और पैसे वाले कागज़ मना · max 7 kg · postage, registration, inquiry fee से छूट; AD की fee लगती है · air surcharge printed papers जितना · शर्त टूटी: आगे नहीं, sender को वापस, inland letter या parcel postage, फिर से post हो सकता है.
  - *Book का मतलब (Part 4, on screen while the voice says its two lines):* किसी भी भाषा में पूरा volume, या volume का भाग या हिस्सा · संगीत, map, chart या plan की अलग से छपी sheet · सामग्री पूरी या मुख्य रूप से पढ़ने की सामग्री, paintings, photographs, diagrams या ऐसी ही चीज़ें · students के notes के लिए खाली जगह चलती है.
  - *Gyan Post content conditions (Part 5, built in two groups as the voice names them):* 1 नियमित अंतराल पर छपने वाले publications नहीं (weekly, fortnightly, monthly, हर 2 महीने, quarterly, half-yearly, या कोई और नियमित अंतराल) · 2 business या commercial किताबें नहीं · 3 चिट्ठी या निजी संदेश, न अंदर न ऊपर · 4 हर किताब में printer या publisher का नाम · 5 advertisements नहीं; incidental announcements या किताबों की list चलती है · 6 price list, bills, supply orders नहीं; इसी किताब का invoice या bill चलता है · 7 अक्षर सिर्फ छपाई या हाथ से; पाने वाले का नाम, तारीख, sender या मालिक का नाम-पता, भेंट के initials.
  - *Gyan Post size (Part 5):* वज़न 300 g से 5 kg · Roll: लंबाई 100–800 mm · Roll: लंबाई + 2 × diameter 170–1000 mm · Roll के अलावा: minimum 140 × 90 mm (± 2 mm), maximum 600 × 300 × 300 mm · बिना बंद envelope: 140 × 90 mm से 353 × 250 mm (± 2 mm). A slate-struck "100 × 70 mm" pill beside the minimum when the voice names the trap.
  - *Gyan Post tariff (Part 6), header "Rates exclude applicable taxes · Schedule I, Table X":* 300 g तक ₹20 · 301–500 g ₹25 · 501–1000 g ₹35 · 1001–2000 g ₹50 · 2001–3000 g ₹65 · 3001–4000 g ₹80 · 4001–5000 g ₹100. The voice names only the first and last rows, then "बीच के slabs, table में हैं"; the whole table stays up so the student can pause on it. Hold it through the slab examples (500 g ₹25, 501 g ₹35, 2000 g ₹50) with a yellow band moving to each row; a slate-struck "299 g" chip beside the first row during the trap.
  - *Double deficiency (Part 8):* the seven breach conditions as the material lists them (this is not the same seven as Part 5; head it "शर्त टूटने की सात बातें"): नियमित publications · बाहर Gyan Post · advertisements · printer या publisher का नाम · commercial किताबें · अक्षर और लेख · price lists, bills, supply orders. Group the six book conditions together and set "बाहर Gyan Post" apart, as the voice does. Formula card: वसूली = 2 × (Parcel Retail का सही postage − दिया गया postage). Worked sum: 600 g · Gyan Post ₹35 · Parcel Retail (local) ₹48 · कमी ₹48 − ₹35 = ₹13 · वसूली 2 × ₹13 = ₹26 (taxes से पहले); slate-struck pills "₹96" and "₹70".
  - *Two breach rules (Part 8), compare cards:* Literature for the Blind — संबंधित postal item के postage का दोगुना · Gyan Post — Parcel Retail माना जाए, कमी का दोगुना, delivery पर.
  - *Five items (Part 8):* 2 kg Braille किताब → Literature for the Blind, postage नहीं, हवा से airmail fee · 2 kg competitive exam textbook (आम छपाई) → Gyan Post, ₹50 + taxes · monthly exam journal → Gyan Post नहीं · नेत्रहीन को भेंट की आम छपाई की किताब → Literature for the Blind नहीं, जिस product में भेजी जाए उसकी शर्तें · Braille पत्रिका → Literature for the Blind हो सकती है. Then the checking order: 1 असली सामान → 2 सही category → 3 वज़न, size, packing, बाहर की details, charges.
  - *Registration and insurance (Part 7), a three-column card while the voice gives the three firm points:* आम नियम — registration और insurance, Speed Post तक सीमित; Speed Post registration ₹5 + GST · Gyan Post का नियम — registration, POD, insurance तीनों का नाम · पक्की बातें — tracking ज़रूरी; POD अलग से, अलग fee; सिर्फ tracking से registered या insured नहीं.
- **Where a pause matters.** Hold one second of silence after "नहीं।" in the Part 1 trap, after "कुल 1020 mm।" in the roll sum, and after "उसका दोगुना: ₹26।". Hold the tariff table and the five-items table for their full narration; the receipt list gets two spoken lines over it.
- **Colours by meaning.** Yellow band for the facts that differ between the two services (100 × 70 vs 140 × 90; double postage vs double deficiency; free postage vs airmail fee). Green for "allowed" exceptions (title, key, return label; own invoice; presentation initials). Slate strike-through for the traps (ordinary print to a blind reader; 299 g; ₹96 and ₹70; 100 × 70 mm for Gyan Post). No India Post logo or emblem on any card.
- **For the owner to check.**
  - The Gyan Post provision names registration, proof of delivery and insurance as services its sender may obtain on payment, while the general provisions from 1 October 2025 tie registration and insurance to Speed Post, and how the two are added to a Gyan Post booking is not spelled out. The script states the Gyan Post clause and the three clear points (tracking compulsory; POD asked and paid for separately; tracking alone ≠ registered or insured) without saying how registration or insurance is added.
  - The material's breach list for Gyan Post (the seven conditions that make a packet India Post Parcel – Retail) names the outside "Gyan Post" inscription but not the personal-correspondence condition, which is one of the seven content conditions in Part 5. The script teaches the breach list as the material gives it, plus "अपात्र सामान". Please confirm whether a personal letter inside a Gyan Post packet is also recovered as double deficiency.
