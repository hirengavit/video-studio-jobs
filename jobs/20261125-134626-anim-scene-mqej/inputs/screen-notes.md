- **Opening and key terms:** title card with a parcel icon: "Cash on Delivery (COD)" / "वितरण पर नकद भुगतान", subtitle "Value Payable Post · VP / VPP". Postmark card "1877" and "16 December 2024". Document card (Georgia, white): "Cash on delivery (COD) is a value-added postal service in which the Post Office collects a specified amount from the addressee when delivering an item and remits the amount to the sender." Source beneath: "Post Office Regulations 2024, Reg. 135". Gist below labelled "सार": "delivery पर वसूली, sender को भुगतान". Key-term cards: Sender / प्रेषक; Addressee / प्राप्तकर्ता; Remit / धन प्रेषण; Bona fide order / असली order; Retention / रोककर रखना; Demurrage / विलंब शुल्क; Poste Restante / डाकघर में रखी डाक; Snowball System (name only, no definition: the material gives none). Map card lists the nine parts as numbered Hindi lines, same wording as spoken.
- **Tables on screen (each held while the voice goes through it, about one row per spoken line):** (1) Recovery limits, source "Admin. instructions on Parcel, 27 Dec 2024": "Retail COD · Speed Post Parcel / India Post Parcel · ₹10,000 per item" and "Contractual COD · Speed Post Parcel / India Post Parcel · ₹50,000 per item". (2) COD fee, source "Post Office Regulations 2024, Schedule IV, Table III": "₹6,500 तक (6,500 समेत) · वसूली का 1.6%" and "₹6,500 से ऊपर · ₹100 + 6,500 से ऊपर वाली रकम का 1%"; under it in grey: "Postage, charges, tax अलग". (3) Complaint deadlines, source "Post Office Regulations 2024": "Item न मिला · booking के 15 दिन बाद, 60 दिन के अंदर"; "अंदर damage या loss · item मिलने के 2 दिन के अंदर"; "Compensation claim · posting के 60 दिन बाद नहीं".
- **Lists on screen:** Documents linked with a collection (8, in a grid of two screens of four, held over the two lines that introduce them): legal documents; bonds; insurance policies; promissory notes; railway goods receipts; parcel receipts; bills of lading; bills for collection. Complaint channels (5, one per spoken line): suggestion and complaints book; Post Office web portal; social media; toll-free numbers; written complaint. eMO steps for COD Retail (4 small steps): item number डालें → data निकालें → details जाँचें → eMO book करें.
- **Fee worked examples:** one calculation card per example, built line by line on the spoken numbers, result on a green pill: ₹5,000 → 5,000 × 1.6 ÷ 100 = ₹80; ₹6,500 → 6,500 × 1.6 ÷ 100 = ₹104 (yellow band on "6,500 समेत"); ₹20,000 → 20,000 − 6,500 = 13,500 → 1% = ₹135 → ₹100 + ₹135 = ₹235; ₹50,000 → 50,000 − 6,500 = 43,500 → 1% = ₹435 → ₹100 + ₹435 = ₹535. The wrong method for ₹20,000 (₹100 + 1% of 20,000 = ₹300) appears once in slate with a strike-through and a slate cross, only in Part 5. A final four-box card: वसूली की रकम · COD fee · Postage · Tax.

- **For the owner to check:** (1) Snowball System: the material excludes its coupons and tickets but never says what the scheme is, so the voice gives no definition; add one line if the source has it. (2) Lost-item compensation: here an item without final delivery status is treated as lost at the later of 60 days from booking or 30 days from the complaint, and compensation is paid within 10 days after that. The Registration lecture said compensation is settled within 30 days of the complaint or claim. Both are taught as the material gives them; please check how the two periods fit, so one line can join them.
