# Packing, sealing and posting: the plan

## The plan

**Promise:** By the end, the viewer knows how an article must be packed, closed or sealed, and through which route it must be posted, with the exact numbers and the traps the papers build on them.

**Opening (R1):** Name the topic plainly: packing, sealing and posting. One line of background: these rules sit in the Post Office Regulations 2024, in force from 16 December 2024, and older instructions survive only where they agree with them. The whole picture in two lines: pack so the article, the staff, other mail and the equipment are safe; then post it by the route its service needs. Then the 10 parts, numbered. No hook, no story.

**Chapters, in the material's own order (big to small, idea before trap):**

1. **Topic and big picture:** the topic, the legal base and its date, the two-line picture, the 10 parts.
2. **Part 1 · Packing का मकसद और मज़बूत cover:** what packing means (collection, sorting, transport, delivery), what the cover protects (contents, staff, other mail, equipment), pressure, friction and handling, a loose wrap that hides the contents is not packing, the four cover rules, a non-conforming India Post Parcel is refused for booking (sender or post office may pack it), 1,408 Parcel Packaging Units. A short recap of what the series already taught: wax-cloth inside with strong paper outside, date stamps of at least 2 offices (posting and destination), stamps on the article itself at the top right of the address side (franking too), higher denominations so fewer stamps. Exam angle.
3. **Part 2 · Letter envelope: बंद करना और size:** continuous closure on all sides, no gum residue, no metal clips or staples, the flap positions, a level surface, bending into a U shape. Specs apply to letters other than rolls and not to parcels: 140 × 90 mm to 458 × 324 mm, ±2 mm, 0.15–0.5 mm thick, at least 70 GSM. GSM explained, and the trap: GSM is not thickness and not the letter's weight.
4. **Part 3 · Window envelope, processing की जगह और charge:** what a window envelope is, only the address block with its barcode shows, contents must not shift; window size and edge distances; the three window materials; logo top left, stamps or frank top right 10 mm from the address; the five processing-space numbers; breach of the conditions means double the postage after counting what was paid, with a worked example (assumed amounts) and the trap.
5. **Recap · Part 1 से Part 3:** four keywords.
6. **Part 4 · Liquids, fragile और खास सामान:** the cover's three jobs; double receptacle with its steps (metal or strong wood box, sawdust or bran, enough to absorb all the liquid); the glass-bottle-with-tight-cap example; fragile vs perishable with the ceramic cup example; the Post Office takes no special precautions for fragile articles; insurance for these contents covers loss only, same fee scale; cloth and woollens; articles of great value (general rule); strong smells like asafetida in an airtight tin or metal case; live bees; dangerous goods and the trap that a strong box does not lift a prohibition; air-only vs total prohibition; osmic acid.
7. **Part 5 · Sealing: ordinary और insured:** closure vs an insurance seal that shows interference; sealing wax on unregistered mail and the tissue-paper precaution in hot climates; detailed insured packing sits in administrative instructions, so fine wax is the method taught, not the only lawful one; the insured letter method (strong cover, identical fine-wax seals with one private mark, every flap or seam, tied ends, the thread under the central seal); envelopes it excludes; sender labels vs official labels; the insured parcel (wax or lead with a private mark, every joint or flap, tied string); gold or silver bullion or coins in a strong wooden or metal case, and the stout-cardboard trap; the private device rules; room for official labels and the tied-label exception; stamps still on the article.
8. **Part 6 · Valuable चीज़ें और insurance:** what must go only insured (coins, bullion, precious stones, jewellery, gold or silver articles, currency or bank notes) and why; the four definitions (gold or silver articles, coins, currency notes, jewellery and watches); declare the real value; insurance on Speed Post items from 1 October 2025 at identified offices, maximum ₹5,00,000 from 1 April 2026, ₹20,000 for currency notes; never above the real value.
9. **Recap · Part 4 से Part 6:** four keywords.
10. **Part 7 · Pins, खुले articles और Book Post:** why pins are dangerous; pinned article found at the posting office goes back to the sender at once, found later loses its pins and goes through the RLO (what the RLO is); suspected injurious mail is detained where found and reported at once to police, postal and other designated authorities; Book Post must stay open to examination, secure is not the same as sealed; an unpaid letter found open is closed by the Post Office, which gives no sender the right to post a letter open.
11. **Part 8 · Letter box या counter:** the service decides, not size (the Speed Post letter example); the letter box list with full prepayment and the blind-literature exemption; small packets out from 1 January 2026 (short, taught before); where letter boxes stand, including Mobile Post Offices and steamers; what an accountable item is; counter only, receipt to the person presenting; every India Post Parcel is accountable; a large ordinary article handed in at the window stays ordinary; the other authorised routes (franchisee outlet, kiosk with the Regional Head, web portal or app) and Click N Book (short).
12. **Part 9 · Letter box से बाहर रहने वाले articles:** the table; registered newspaper, Periodical Post and franked items count as unpaid if dropped in; paying and posting are separate (franked mail's office and day, short); recovery: double the prepaid rate or double the deficiency, at least ₹1, from addressee or, if returned, from sender (short); Registration as an addressee-specific Speed Post add-on; words written on a cover book nothing.
13. **Recap · Part 7 से Part 9:** four keywords.
14. **Part 10 · Special letter boxes, समय और booking पूरी करना:** "For letters only" takes letters and postcards; special boxes and "liable to detention"; Quick Mail Service needs the 6-digit PIN under the town; post early; the 30-minute rule counted from mail closing, with the 17:00 → 16:30 example (short, taught before); service indications support booking; COD needs an agreement; high-value stamps on airmail defaced before the presenter; India Post Parcel inscription, declaration, "merchandise"; postage prepaid, the Director General's exception; receipt to the presenter; the item is the sender's property until delivery; cancellation rules (short, taught before) and the 15 July 2026 date; the order: check, protect, close, choose the route.
15. **याद रखने वाली बातें:** six points, the material's key points first, each as the correct fact.
16. **Close:** one warm line from the counter.

Every part of the material has a place: the legal framework (1), safe packing and stamps (2), envelopes (3–4), special contents (6), sealing (7), valuables and insurance including the MCQ meanings (8), pins and open articles (10), posting routes and the extra facts on letter boxes, Parcel Packaging Units and Click N Book (2, 11), articles kept out of letter boxes (12), special boxes, timing, parcel booking, ownership and cancellation (14). Osmic acid appears twice in the material and is taught once (6).

**Ending:** A plain recap of the six points that matter most, then one warm line. No mention of other lectures.
