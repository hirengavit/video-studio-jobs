# Packing, sealing and posting — plan

## The plan

**Promise:** How an article must be packed, closed or sealed, and handed in, under the Post Office Regulations 2024, with the old Guide rules the papers still ask and the traps in each.

**Opening (R1):** The topic named plainly: packing, sealing and posting of inland mail. One line of background: since 16 December 2024 these rules are in the Post Office Regulations 2024; before that they were in Post Office Guide Part I. The whole picture in four short steps: check the contents can go, pack so they and the other mail are safe, close the article the way its class needs, then post it by the right route. Then the map of seven parts. No hook, no story, no mention of other lectures.

**Chapters, in the material's own order:**

1. **Topic और पूरी तस्वीर** — topic, the 2024 Regulations, the four-step picture.
2. **Map** — the seven parts, numbered.
3. **Part 1 · Packing का मकसद और मज़बूत cover (Regulations 25 and 96)** — what packing must do (contents, staff, other mail, equipment); journey stresses; a loosely wrapped article is not packed; a non-conforming India Post Parcel is refused for booking, sender or office may pack it; the four strong-cover conditions; short recap of wax-cloth inside, strong paper outside, 2 date stamps (posting and destination); stamps on the article itself, top right, also for franking; higher-value stamps to keep the count small; old vs new: Guide and 1933 Rules → 2024 Rules and Regulations from 16 December 2024, older instructions only where consistent.
4. **Part 2 · Letter envelope: बंद करना और नाप (Regulation 88)** — continuous closure on all sides, no gum residue, no clips or staples (trap: a staple is not continuous closure); flap positions; level surface; bends into a U shape; the spec table (140 × 90, 458 × 324, ±2 mm, 0.15–0.5 mm, 70 GSM) and that it is for envelopes, not rolls or parcels; GSM meaning and the GSM-vs-thickness trap; window envelopes (only the address block and barcode visible, size 35–50 by 80–125, distances 15 left, 40 top, 15 bottom, three window materials); processing space (logo top left, stamps top right, 10 mm gap, 15 mm at the bottom, QR code 5 mm clear and 18 × 18, logo 25 × 50, window code 60 × 10); breach of Regulation 87 or 88 → double postage with what was paid counted, with a simple worked example.
5. **पहला recap** — five keywords from Parts 1–2.
6. **Part 3 · सामान के हिसाब से packing (Regulation 25)** — one wrapping doesn't suit everything; liquids and things that liquefy: double receptacle, three steps, metal or strong wood, absorbent enough for all the liquid, its job is to stop spread; the material's glass-bottle example; fragile and perishable with the ceramic-cup example; the Guide's warning on fragile articles; insurance for liquids, perishables and fragile only against loss, same fee scale; cloth, valuables, strong-smelling (asafetida, airtight metal case), live bees; trap: safe packing does not make prohibited contents admissible, dangerous goods defined, air-only vs total prohibition; old vs new: osmic acid packing and labels → Regulation 25 prohibitions since 16 December 2024.
7. **Part 4 · Sealing: आम और insured (Regulation 133)** — closure vs insurance seal; sealing wax advice and tissue paper in hot climates; Regulation 133 leaves insured packing details to administrative instructions, so wax is the traditional method, not the only one; the five-step fine-wax method for insured letters; excluded envelopes (black or coloured border, transparent panel), sender labels vs official labels; insured parcel method; bullion and coins in a wooden or metal case (trap: stout cardboard); private device rules; space for official labels, tied-label exception, stamps still on the article; valuables only insured, declare value; the Guide's clause 17 meanings (gold or silver articles, coins, currency notes, jewellery); insurance on Speed Post items up to ₹5 lakh, notes ₹20,000, not above real value; old vs new: ₹1 lakh accountable item → Speed Post item from 1 October 2025 → ₹5 lakh from 1 April 2026.
8. **दूसरा recap** — five keywords from Parts 3–4.
9. **Part 5 · Pins वाली डाक और खुली भेजी जाने वाली डाक (Regulations 88 and 92)** — why pins are dangerous; three situations, three actions (sender at once; remove pins and return via RLO; detain and refer to the Head of the Circle); Book Post may go open for examination; secure packing is not always sealed packing; trap: an unfastened Book Post cover is not an improperly closed letter; old vs new: the old unpaid-letter closing rule → continuous closure under Regulation 88.
10. **Part 6 · Post कहाँ करें: letter box या counter (Regulation 22)** — service and article decide, not size; short recap of the letter-box categories, full prepayment, literature for the blind exemption, small packets removed from 1 January 2026; accountable item meaning, counter only, receipt to the person presenting; all India Post Parcels accountable; large ordinary articles at the window are not made accountable; franchisee outlet, self-booking kiosk (Regional Head designates), web portal or app; the excluded items table; payment method vs posting method for franked mail (short recap of same-date or next-working-day presentation); unpaid recovery (double rate, double deficiency, ₹1 minimum, addressee or sender); old vs new: Guide's parcel-in-letter-box rule → accountable parcels, registration as a Speed Post add-on from 1 October 2025; writing a service name on a cover does not book it.
11. **तीसरा recap** — five keywords from Parts 5–6.
12. **Part 7 · Special letter boxes, समय और booking पूरी करना (Regulations 22 to 24)** — "For letters only" takes letters and postcards; class boxes and liability to detention; Quick Mail Service full PIN, 6 digits; short recap of the 30-minute rule counted from mail closing, with the 17:00 → 16:30 example, and today's notified hours; service indications at the top of the address side; airmail high-value stamps defaced in the presenter's presence; India Post Parcel inscription, value additions, declaration, merchandise, form set by instructions; prepayment and the Director General's exception; receipt to the presenter; sender's property until delivery unless seized; Regulation 23A cancellation (self-booked before acceptance, counter booking on the same day, office cancels wrong data entry the same day, refund under instructions) and that it is not recall.
13. **पुराने Guide clauses और आज के नियम** — the clause map on screen, key pairs said by subject (clause numbers only on screen); the Guide's letter-box list including Mobile Post Offices and steamers; 1,408 Parcel Packaging Units; Click N Book.
14. **याद रखने वाली बातें** — seven plain points, the material's key points first, each confusion said only as the correct fact.
15. **आखिरी बात** — one warm line.

**Earlier lectures:** stamps on the article, the 2 date stamps, wax-cloth, the letter-box list, small packets, accountable items, other posting modes, franked-mail presentation, unpaid recovery, the 30-minute rule and Regulation 23A were taught before; here they get short recaps, said as plain facts, never contradicting those lectures and never naming them.

**Ends:** the seven-point recap, then one warm line about safe packing protecting the mail and the hands that carry it.
