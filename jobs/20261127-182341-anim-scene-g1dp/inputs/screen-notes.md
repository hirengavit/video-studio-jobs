- **Document cards (Georgia, white, source beneath; Hindi gist labelled "सार (gist)"):** (1) "Post Office Act, 2023 · Section 3": services prescribed by the Central Government. (2) "Post Office Rules, 2024 · Rule 3": citizen-centric services authorised by the Central Government; services offered by State or Local Governments; services through collaboration with public or private entities. "Rule 4": the Director General may introduce, modify or discontinue products and enter into collaborations. "Rule 2(1)(c)": Circle Head means the Chief Postmaster General concerned or an officer exercising those powers in the Circle. (3) "Post Office Regulations, 2024 · Reg. 105 Retail Post", four points as spoken (departmental products by administrative instructions; third-party products by agreement with public or private sector; Circle Head enters into agreements; charges and conditions by administrative instructions). (4) "Reg. 104 e-Payment": collection of bills or other payments for a third party; agreement with the concerned postal authority. (5) "Reg. 174(3)": Director General issues instructions on charges, operating procedures and accounting procedures for partnerships. (6) Aadhaar card: identity subject to authentication or offline verification; not proof of citizenship or domicile; not conclusive proof of date of birth. Hold each card for the whole explanation, highlight moving through it.
