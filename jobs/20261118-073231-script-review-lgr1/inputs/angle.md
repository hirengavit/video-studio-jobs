# Department of Posts structure, circles and their headquarters — plan

## The plan

**Promise.** By the end, the student can draw the whole Department of Posts from the top down: who heads it and what each top post does, what the Postal Services Board is, how a Circle breaks into Regions, Divisions and Sub-divisions, all 23 Circles with their headquarters, the Circles that also serve another state or UT, the Base Circle, the special offices, and the Boards with look-alike names. Every exam trap on this topic is taught once, at its own idea.

**Opening (R1).** No hook. The topic named plainly; one line of background (India Post, under the Ministry of Communications, roots in the unified postal administration of 1 October 1854); the whole picture in two sentences (the top in Delhi, then 23 civil Circles plus 1 Base Circle, each Circle into Regions, Divisions and Sub-divisions). Then the map of eight numbered parts.

**Order.** Big to small, in the material's own order. A few stray facts from the material's closing "exam facts" block are placed in the part they belong to (Act dates with the legal framework; Dalhousie, RMS 1854, APS 1856 and Postal Manual 1860 with the history; the "D" Section with the Directorate; IFW with the Financial Adviser; RMS Sub-division head and wider definitions with the field structure; network numbers at the end of the field structure; 56 APO and 99 APO coverage and the Defence Minister with APS; DRC and "Post Info" with CEPT; founding years with the Directorates; Lakshadweep's old name with the jurisdiction table; Guide's 7 sections with the Guide).

1. **Topic and the whole picture** — what the Department is, the top and the field in two sentences, the eight parts.
2. **Part 1 · The top: Secretary, DGPS and the law** — services offered; Communications Minister; Secretary (Posts) as head and PSB Chairperson; HQ Dak Bhawan, Sansad Marg, New Delhi 110001; Postal Directorate and its work; "D" Section as Central Bag Office. Secretary vs DGPS (policy and assigned duties vs operations and assigned administration; duties revised 27 March 2026; not every administrative matter goes to DGPS). Appointments (President for Secretary; Central Government for DGPS, including an authorised officer). DG's powers (regulations on activities, charges, terms; Rule 4: start, change, stop products and collaborate). Question wording: Head of Department, Chairperson PSB, head of operations. Legal framework (Act 2023 with its passing dates and commencement, repeal of 1898 Act; Rules and Regulations 2024 from 16 December 2024, Rules replacing 1933 Rules with the savings line). History (Dalhousie and stamps, RMS 1854, APS 1856, Postal Manual 1860, Rafi Ahmed Kidwai first Communications Minister, Minister of State post, the vision). Key fact said twice: Secretary heads the Department, DGPS heads operations.
3. **Part 2 · Postal Services Board** — apex policy body under Rule 5; apex management body; perpetual succession and common seal in plain words first; Rule 6 gazette power, no fixed number in the rule; ex-officio Chairperson; 1 Chairperson + 7 Members (table on screen; two named aloud); portfolio names can change; 20 September 2024 rename to Member (Coordination); Financial Adviser as permanent invitee, AS and FA is one post (trap), listed as JS and FA, FA heads IFW; Secretary (PSB) vs Secretary (Posts) (trap); DDGs, Directors, ADGs at HQ; DDG is not DGPS. **Recap card 1.**
4. **Part 3 · Circle to Sub-division** — Circle, CPMG, Regions, Divisions, Sub-divisions and why; the heads table (with RMS Sub-division head IRM or ASRM); one illustrative chain example (a BO's supervision up to CPMG); Rule 2 definitions (Circle Head, Regional Head, Regional Director, wide Divisional Head; usual answer SPOs or SSPOs); wider meanings of PMG, Superintendent, Director (Postal Services); Postal Division vs RMS Division, RMS full form and all four routes, SPOs/SSPOs vs SRM/SSRM (trap); 2026 common structure: Head of Region (PMG or Director level), Head of Logistics (what it coordinates, reports to Head of Circle, Member (Operations) reviews, what it controls, Head of Sales and Head of Transportation report to it, JAG or SAG, PMG or Director (Logistics) designation, no national Logistics Directorate), Postal Divisions stay the main unit, implementation letter 15 September 2026 with 30 September 2026 deadline; control vs appointment (trap), 17 February 2025 powers, Sub-division head as engaging authority for ABPM and Dak Sevak only where assigned, CPMG fixes Division and Sub-division jurisdiction and HQ with the Circle IFA; 23 Postal Accounts Offices; network numbers (table). Key fact twice: CPMG, PMG, SPOs/SSPOs, ASP/IPO.
5. **Part 4 · 23 Circles and their headquarters** — meaning of headquarters; HQ need not be the capital (trap); full list on a table; the five pairs said aloud (Gujarat; Haryana–Punjab; Andhra–Telangana; Assam–North East; Karnataka–Kerala) and Mysuru's real link; Jammu and Kashmir (Srinagar as HQ, office and letters also from Jammu, one Circle); old names Bangalore, Trivandrum, Orissa. **Recap card 2.**
6. **Part 5 · Circles that cover more than one state** — meaning of jurisdiction and same-boundary Circles; table of nine; Gujarat with DNH and DD (merger 26 January 2020; Goa trap) with the material's Daman example; North East's 6 states, Assam and Sikkim outside (trap); West Bengal, Kerala (Lakshadweep, old name Laccadive), Maharashtra, Punjab, J&K with Ladakh; Puducherry split three ways and the default answer Tamil Nadu; postal arrangement does not change constitutional status.
7. **Part 6 · Base Circle and Army Postal Service** — APS as Base Circle; 23 + 1; head ADG APS, Major General; APS Directorate New Delhi; deputation in plain words; 75% other ranks only (trap); Defence Minister; CBPOs with APO, city and PIN; APO, FPO full forms; first digit 9; 56 APO and 99 APO coverage (the 8 north-eastern states made sense of); CBPOs link civil and army networks; FPOs; 2 CBPOs is not the whole network (trap). **Recap card 3.**
8. **Part 7 · Special offices, technology and training** — functional vs geographical offices; PLI Directorate (Director PLI Kolkata trap); Parcel Directorate and CGM; Business Development as the older name; CCS and RB Directorate and Secretary (PSB) as its Head of Department from 19 August 2026, no separate budget; founding years and places; PSD, CSD, Group B charge, MMS, Foreign Post and Controller of Foreign Mails; CEPT (full form, Mysuru, Head at Bengaluru, other offices, DRC since 15 May 2015, Post Info app); RAKNPA (Ghaziabad, what training); 6 PTCs on a table, Vadodara said aloud, CEPT vs PTC in Mysuru (trap); 7th PTC foundation stone at Shivpuri, 2 September 2026; PO Guide Parts I–IV, Part I's 7 sections starting with control, Guide applies where consistent.
9. **Part 8 · Boards with look-alike names** — "Board" is not always PSB; five Boards with purpose and head; PSSWB welfare scope; Secretary as Executive Vice-Chairman of the Staff Welfare Board; Sports Board's Co-Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson; the Minister-vs-Secretary trap.
10. **याद रखने वाली बातें** — the key points of each section first, each confusion said only as the correct fact.
11. **Close** — one warm line, no mention of other lectures.

**Ending.** A calm teacher's sum-up of the points that matter most, then one warm line.
