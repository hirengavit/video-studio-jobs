# Script checklist: Postal Exams

1. **Does the lecture open by naming the topic plainly, with the big picture, within 40 seconds, so a newcomer knows what they are studying?** Read the first 100 words. A hook, puzzle, surprise question or story at the start fails.
2. **Does it teach in a natural order, big to small, each idea before its exceptions and traps?** Mark any trap or special case that comes before its idea, any story or single example running through the lecture, and any repeated catchphrase.
3. **Is there a map of the parts, with names matching the section labels used later?**
4. **Does every part get to its exam angle quickly, with an example where the idea needs one?** Mark any part missing its exam angle; any hard or easily confused idea left without an example; any of the material's own examples left out or put in the wrong part; any added example that brings in a new fact, number or name as real; and any part padded with examples the viewers don't need.
5. **Is the current position taught as plain fact, with no "as of <month year>", "इस समय" or "… की स्थिति में" qualifiers in the voice or on screen?** Dates of events (when something began, changed or was replaced) stay, once, where they start. A lecture made from material needs no margin sources (the material is the source; check 7). Without material, every number, limit, rate, rule number, section, date and exam detail needs a source in the margin.
6. **Is the current rule stated as plain fact, with no old-vs-new card, comparison or "earlier" framing in the voice or on screen?** Where a rule was replaced, only the new one stays. A rule that did not change is stated as current. Without material: check against the 2023 Act, the 2024 Rules and Regulations, Speed Post with registration as an add-on (2025), the MO limit of ₹10,000, the PA/SA pattern (local-language paper removed 2024) and DEST key presses.
7. **Is nothing invented, including "exam में आया था" claims?** Each must cite a real past paper with its exam and year, or be cut. For a lecture made from material: is every fact, number, date, name and exam claim in the material? Mark anything that isn't and cut it.
8. **Is everything in the material in the script?** Go through the material heading by heading and list every concept, fact, number, date, name, definition, distinction, example, correction and trap that is missing or changed; put each one back.
9. **Are only the new or tricky terms explained, and is nothing the viewers already know explained at length?** Mark any passage that explains everyday postal terms or background the viewers know; it should be cut or shortened.
10. **Is it real Hinglish: Hindi in Devanagari, English terms in English letters, no transliterated English, no shuddh Hindi?** Search the script for Devanagari spellings of English words (स्पीड, पोस्ट ऑफिस, अकाउंट) and fix them.
11. **Are most sentences under 12 words, one idea each, ending in "।" or "?"?** Check a sample of 20 sentences.
12. **Are rules, sections, definitions and formulae shown in their exact words where the material gives them, otherwise as a labelled "सार" gist?** The voice must not read the card aloud word for word. Never look up text the material doesn't give.
13. **Is the lecture teaching only?** No quick-check questions, MCQs, practice set or "video रोकिए, comment में लिखिए".
14. **Is there a recap at least every 7 minutes, and a short closing recap of the five or so points that matter most, the material's key points first?**
15. **Are there no more than three new facts in any minute, with the key fact of each part said twice?**
16. **Are pay, GDS status, union and service disputes handled neutrally, with sources?**
17. **Is the script free of clickbait, fear, paper-leak talk, course plugs and Telegram plugs?** Search against off-limits.json, in Hindi and English.
18. **Does it fit the series (R14)?** Search for "lecture", "पिछल", "अगल" and "पहले देख": any line that mentions another lecture fails; teach the fact plainly instead. Against `inputs/earlier/`: mark any topic an earlier lecture taught in full that is taught again at length here (cut it to a short recap), and any line that contradicts, or sounds as if it contradicts, an earlier lecture (add the one line that shows how both fit).
19. **Is the tone warm and respectful ("आप"), with a small smile once or twice, and no mocking of any cadre, Circle or community?**
20. **Are the material's key points (its "Prelims focus" lists) the strongest parts of the lecture?** Each one should get a clear explanation and appear in the closing recap; background gets a line. Search for "prelims", "mains", "प्रारंभिक" and "मुख्य परीक्षा": departmental postal exams have none, so any of these words in the voice or on screen fails.
21. **Is each trap taught once, as the correct fact (R3)?** Count how often each wrong version (a wrong number, a wrong office, "X नहीं") is said. More than once fails: after the first time, only the correct fact is said.
22. **Where the material gives two figures for the same thing, does the script teach only the current one (R9)?** The older figure is not mentioned.
23. **Is every definition from an Act or Rule said in everyday words first, with no translated legal sentence in the voice (R7)?** Mark each one that sounds like a translation.
24. **Is every list of more than five items on a screen table, with the voice giving only the count, the groups and the one or two items that matter (R11)?** Mark any list read out in full.
25. **Does every line of the material that would puzzle a student have its one-line explanation from the material, or a "For the owner to check" entry in Production notes (R9)?** Look especially at counts that don't match their lists and traps given without a reason.
26. **Is each rule, regulation, clause or section number spoken at most once, and no old Guide clause or "पुराने Guide" reference in the voice (R7)?** Search the narration for "clause" and "पुराने"; a number that repeats or is stacked in one sentence fails.
