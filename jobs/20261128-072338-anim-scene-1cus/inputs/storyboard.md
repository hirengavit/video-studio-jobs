# Storyboard · TD and MIS (Postal Exams, POSB)

No character in this channel: no host rig, no person drawn. Every scene is an animated lecture slide on the cream canvas (#fbf7ee) under the app's fixed frame (grey section label top left, red series pill top right; never drawn by the scene, never animated). Content area: x 100–1820, y 150–1000 (the header owns y 0–130). Postal terms in English (Inter bold, 88–120 px) with the Hindi translation beneath (Noto Sans Devanagari, 48–60 px, warm grey or red); everything else Hindi only, never under 44 px. Max five items on screen; earlier points fade to #6f6a63 when a screen gets busy. A new picture or a new build about every 4.5 s; key facts hold ≥ 4 s; a rule card holds through its whole explanation with highlights moving through it. Moves used: draw, write, pop, fade, slide, type, count, grow, move, pulse, color, out, words, tracking, reveal, stamp, highlight, underline, strike, swap. Never: smear, ticker, scatter, spin, float, wiggle, shake, bounce, flip, letters.

## Continuity

- No character anywhere. One world: the cream paper deck. The two recurring scheme icons keep their colours everywhere: TD = white passbook (td-passbook-white) on the LEFT, MIS = red passbook on the RIGHT whenever both appear (screen direction: TD left, MIS right, for every compare card, table column and recap row).
- Inside a chapter, scenes cut (`cut`); a scene that keeps the previous card on screen says `Continues: sNNN` and starts with that card exactly as it ended. A new chapter (section label changes) arrives with `wipe` from the right (0.6 s); recaps arrive with `fade` (0.5 s). The first scene (s001) is a `cut` with the hook already on screen.
- Colour meanings never change: red = cards and headings; yellow = highlight / what's added; green = allowed / correct / ticks; slate strike + cross = wrong option; grey = supporting.
- Rates always carry "1 Oct–31 Dec 2026 · Source: SB Order 12/2026" beneath; limits and fees carry their scheme or SB Order beneath.
- Shared state: none changes across scenes (state.json is {}).
