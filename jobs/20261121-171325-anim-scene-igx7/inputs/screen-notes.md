Section label top left in English (e.g. "PART 5 · INLAND LETTER CARD"), red series pill "PRODUCTS" top right.
- **Tables to build (R11, voice never reads them in full):** the ten numbered Part headings verbatim and in order; letter dimensions (roll and non-roll rows, minimum and maximum, with "Reg. 87 · Post Office Regulations 2024"); the 8 envelope specifications; window materials and margins on a drawn envelope with measured arrows (15 left, 15 bottom, 40 top, 10 mm gap, 15 mm barcode strip); the letter card unfolded and folded sizes with the 3 flaps; the 4 permitted postcard attachments; the seven second-class entries in the Guide sorting taxonomy (book and pattern packets; printed-book packets; periodical packets; blind literature packets; registered newspapers; sample packets; parcels), with 10 total / nine letter-mail / one parcel-mail; mark this as a terminology reference, not the current booking menu; the 3 condition-breach calculations side by side.

Rates (letter ₹5 per 20 g, letter card ₹2.50, postcard 50 paise, airmail ₹2 + ₹1 per 50 g) shown with "Source: Post Office Regulations 2024, Schedule I".
- **Pauses:** no quick checks. Hold a beat of about 1 s after each worked total, and let each envelope recap card hold until its last keyword ticks green, plus one second.
- **Terminology card (not separate tariffs):** Printed postcard: machine-printed text or images, including cyclostyling (stencil duplication). Meghdoot: advertisement beside the address, up to four colours, 140 × 90 mm. Competition: response to a competition in TV, radio, press, a magazine or another medium; not student-only. The supplied history dates consolidation to 20 December 2024. Show one current domestic tariff, not a before/after rate chart.
