## Continuity

No character, so continuity is layout: scenes that carry the same card **cut** (`Continues: sNNN`) and keep every element where it was; the next card in the same part arrives with a **cut**; a new Part (and each recap / remember block) arrives with a **wipe from right** (0.5 s); s001 opens on a cut (first frame already full), s218 fades in. Screen direction: sequences and journeys read left → right; "before → after" and "allowed → forbidden" also left → right. The letter envelope always sits address side up, stamps top right. The camera stays at zoom 1 unless a scene says otherwise, and any push-in returns to 1 by the cut unless the next scene continues it.
