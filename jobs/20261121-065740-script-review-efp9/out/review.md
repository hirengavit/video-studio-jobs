# Script review

## Problems

### Map and section labels
> Screen पर दस parts का map है।

The spoken map drifts from the chapter labels. Keep the ten numbered headings verbatim on screen.
**Fix:** पहले तीन रूप और उनकी शर्तें देखेंगे। फिर mail की classes, हवाई डाक और counter पर वसूली।

### Rollout date
> देश भर में इस व्यवस्था की जानकारी, 7 July 2026 को दी गई।

This is a reporting date, not legal commencement. Keep it on the date card, attributed to the supplied material.
**Fix:** Remove the line.

### Repeated trap wording
> Option में लिखा हो कि barcode लगा है, इसलिए item accountable है, तो गलत।

The previous line teaches the correct fact; remove the repeated wrong option.
**Fix:** Remove the line.

### Film label
> Safety cinematograph film, यानी आग न पकड़ने वाली film का packet।

The prescribed label and classification do not guarantee that a film cannot catch fire.
**Fix:** Safety cinematograph film के packet की अलग पहचान है।

### Charging precision
> और हिसाब में paise आएँ, तो उसे पूरा रुपया गिना जाता है।

A fractional rupee rounds up; the whole amount does not become ₹1.
**Fix:** रकम में paise बचें, तो अगले पूरे रुपये तक बढ़ाइए।

### Legacy postcard terms
> अब तीन पुराने नाम, जो exam में आते हैं।

The old descriptions are exam vocabulary, not standalone products. Move those descriptions and the dated consolidation to production cards; teach the current tariff aloud.
**Fix:** छपा संदेश, advertisement या competition का जवाब, तीनों के लिए यही tariff है। 

### Reply-half scope
> जवाब के लिए इस्तेमाल हुआ postcard भी, postcard की सारी शर्तें पूरी करे।

The current rule is for an ordinary domestic postcard. An attached legacy reply half is not an ordinary card attachment, and Business Reply Service is a separate arrangement.
**Fix:** अलग से भेजा जाने वाला साधारण reply भी postcard की शर्तें पूरी करे।

### Sorting taxonomy versus current product list
> Screen पर उनकी list है, सात items की।

The 10/9/1 list includes Guide-era packet names. Label it as Guide taxonomy, not as a current list of bookable products; do not equate sample packets and small packets.
**Fix:** Guide की sorting terminology में इनकी अलग list है। ये आज की booking categories की list नहीं है।

### Guide structure
> PO Guide Part I के दो sections हैं।

The supplied source identifies two relevant sections, not a claim that the whole Guide has only two.
**Fix:** Guide Part I में इस विषय के दो sections हैं।

### Biological specimen scope
> Perishable biological substances भी letter post से जा सकते हैं।

A legacy packing reference alone does not establish present admissibility. Teach a compliance check, not a general mailing permission.
**Fix:** Biological specimens के लिए, लागू नियमों के तहत अनुमति और packing दोनों जाँचिए। आम letter की तरह इन्हें न लें।

### Open unpaid letter
> Letter prepaid हो, और अच्छी तरह बंद हो।

Distinguish the handling procedure from permission to post an open or unpaid letter.
**Fix:** Letter prepaid हो, और अच्छी तरह बंद हो। खुला unpaid letter मिलने पर, उसे बंद करना booking की अनुमति नहीं है।

## Passed

- The first 100 words name the three products and promise a clear counter decision. The sequence goes from product to dimensions, build and dues; examples follow the rules.
- Opening date, letter limits, window and barcode measures, letter-card limits and postcard tariff were checked against the supplied reference and the relevant India Post/Gazette product texts. The official and supplied rules distinguish all three breach charges.
- Roll arithmetic, the three charge examples and the ₹4 air-mail example reconcile. Current government product pages confirm the letter and card tariffs and the ordinary postcard attachment scope.
- Comparisons teach, not test; no MCQs, answer pauses, sensational claims, past-paper claims, sales prompts or off-limits lines. No cadre, union, pay or dispute discussion.
- Review of prior packing, postage, addressing, letter-box and prohibited-article lessons found expected overlap only in short application recaps; current ordinary postcard attachment scope takes priority over legacy reply-half wording.
- Stage directions belong in Production notes. Large comparison lists have production tables; named recap cards break up the lecture. Some long source-heavy lists remain dense even with visuals.
- English postal terms stay in Latin script; sampled lines are generally short and use ।. Exact supplied labels go on cards; any paraphrase is a labelled gist.

## Weakest part

Part 8 is the densest: sorting priority, airmail surcharge and legacy operational examples are easy to conflate. Show those as distinct categories, and verify current operational applicability before recording the source-dependent examples. The dates of the barcode and postcard-history releases were not independently established from the original documents.
